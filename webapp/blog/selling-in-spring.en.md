When the days get longer, half the Netherlands goes looking for a road bike. If you have a strong ad up right then, with good photos, the right model year and a fair price, you sell fastest. Most of the work you can do now, while the bike is indoors anyway.

![](chart:selling-plan)

## 1. Find out exactly what your bike is

Buyers search by model, and Bikecheck and other sites check your ad against the original. Look your bike up in the [catalogue](/models): the full model, the generation and the original groupset. Not sure of the model year? Check the receipt, or compare the colour with the photos per year on the model page. A wrong year is the first thing that makes a buyer suspicious.

## 2. Get it ready

- Clean the bike, chain and cassette included.
- Replace what's clearly worn: a worn chain or bald tyres cost little but put buyers off.
- Find the receipt. A receipt with the frame number is the best reassurance for buyers that the bike isn't stolen (see [spotting a stolen bike](/blog/spotting-a-stolen-bike)).

## 3. Take the photos

On [Improve my ad](/sell) we take you through the photos buyers want to see in five steps: the whole bike from both sides and from the front at an angle, the derailleur, the chainrings, the shifters, the brakes, the tyres and the frame sticker. Your photos stay in your own browser. Shoot in daylight, against a calm background, with the drivetrain facing the camera.

## 4. Set a fair price

Don't work from what the bike costs new today, nor from what you paid back then, but from the new price of **its model year**. The [value estimator](/value) does that and gives you a range. A price at the top of that range is fine for a bike in top condition; far above it means buyers who use our check get a warning. Read also [how fast a road bike loses value](/blog/how-fast-bikes-lose-value).

## 5. Write the ad

On [Improve my ad](/sell) you paste your draft or the link to your ad. We show what's missing (size, year, groupset, condition) and suggest a new title and text, with the photos in the right order. You also see straight away which verdicts a buyer gets when they check your ad.

> Ready in February? Put the ad online as soon as the first nice weekend is in sight. Then you're at the top when the buyers come.
