Not everyone rides through winter. If your good bike goes into the shed in November, the next few months decide what state it's in come spring, and so what it's worth if you sell it. Rust on the chain, a flat tyre that's stood for months, a battery that died: none of it is expensive on its own, but a buyer sees it, and it pulls your bike from "like new" to "used".

## What condition is worth

Our [value estimator](/value) starts from the new price and the age, then corrects for condition: **like new** counts 5% up, **used** 8% down, **damaged** 40% down.

Take a [Trek Domane SL 5 Gen 5](/models/trek-domane-sl-5-gen-5) (€2,499 new), two years old:

| Condition | Fair price |
|---|---|
| Like new | €1,560 to €1,900 |
| Normal use | €1,480 to €1,810 |
| Used | €1,370 to €1,670 |
| Damaged | €890 to €1,090 |

![](chart:condition-values)

Between like new and used is about **€215**. That's more than a winter's worth of care costs. Damage from corrosion or a bad fall in the shed costs far more.

## Before it goes in

- **Clean it, and dry it.** Road salt keeps working all winter. Wash the frame, the drivetrain and the brakes, and let it dry completely before you put it away.
- **Oil the chain.** Clean first, then a fresh coat of oil, and wipe off what's too much. A dry chain rusts in a damp shed.
- **Charge the battery.** With Di2 or AXS, charge it fully before storage and top it up halfway through. A battery left empty for months can be ruined.
- **Hydraulic brakes:** don't squeeze the lever with a wheel out. Put the pad spacer in if you take wheels off.

## Where it stands

- **Dry and frost-free if you can.** Damp, cold sheds cause condensation, and that gets into bearings and the bottom bracket.
- **Off its tyres.** Hang it from a wheel, or pump the tyres to full pressure and check them every few weeks. A tyre that stands flat for months gets a flat spot and cracks.
- **Don't clamp a carbon frame.** In a work stand, clamp the seatpost, never the top tube.
- **Under a cover, not in plastic.** A breathable cover keeps dust off; plastic holds the moisture in.

## In spring

Before the first ride, or before the [ad goes online](/blog/selling-in-spring): check the chain for wear, look at the brake pads and tyres, and check the battery. And keep the receipts of any service. A buyer who sees the history believes "like new" much sooner.
