Trek is the brand we meet most in ads: in a sample of 72 checked ads, 28 were Treks, and the Domane is a regular among them. It makes sense: it's a bike for long rides on rough roads, with a more upright position and room for wide tyres. Just what many Dutch and Belgian riders are after.

The tricky part is that "Domane" is a whole family. Our [catalogue](/models?q=domane) holds seventy, from €949 to €12,999.

## AL, SL and SLR: the frame

- **Domane AL** is aluminium. The entry bike, now €999 (AL 2, Claris) to €1,799 (AL 5, 105).
- **Domane SL** is carbon (Trek calls it OCLV 500). The most popular one second-hand, now €2,499 to €5,499.
- **Domane SLR** is the lighter OCLV 800 carbon, for the serious rider. Now €6,999 to €12,999.

A **Domane+** is the electric version. It has a motor and battery and so a very different price.

## The number: the groupset

The number after AL, SL or SLR gives the groupset's level: 2 is Claris, 4 Tiagra, 5 is 105, 6 is 105 Di2 or SRAM Rival AXS, 7 is Ultegra Di2 or Force, 9 is Dura-Ace or Red. An "SL 6 eTap" or "SL 6 AXS" is the SRAM version.

## Mind the generation

The generation sets the new price, and that has risen a lot. A **Domane SL 6 Gen 3** cost €3,299 in 2020, €3,999 in 2021 and €4,199 in 2022. An **AL 2 Gen 3** was €729 in 2021. Value an old Domane from today's price and you'll quickly be too high.

![New prices from Trek's archived spec sheets.](chart:domane-sl6-gen3)

**Worked example.** A 2021 Domane SL 6 Gen 3 (€3,999 new) in normal used condition is worth about **€1,620 to €1,980** after five years, according to our [value estimator](/value).

## What to check on a used Domane

- **The IsoSpeed**, the pivot at the seat post that soaks up bumps: ask whether it creaks or has play.
- **The size**: Trek publishes a size chart per model. Check yours in the [size finder](/size).
- **The generation**: the colour in the photos often gives away the year. Paste the ad into the [check](/) and we'll work it out.
