## Carbon: Topstone Carbon

The **Topstone Carbon** has a carbon frame with **Kingpin**, a small pivot behind the seat tube that lets the rear flex a few centimetres. That makes it more comfortable off-road. Now €2,699 to €7,999.

**Lefty** in the name means Cannondale's one-sided suspension fork. Handy on rough tracks, but it needs servicing: ask the seller when the fork was last serviced.

## Gravel prices are falling

Where road bikes kept getting dearer in recent years, the Topstone shows the opposite:

- **Topstone 3**: €1,499 in 2023, 2024 and 2025, the same price three years running.
- **Topstone 1**: €2,449 in 2022, €2,399 in 2024.
- **Topstone Carbon 2 AXS**: €3,999 in 2024, €3,099 in 2025.
- **Topstone Carbon 1 RLE**: €8,999 in 2023, €7,799 in 2024.

![](chart:topstone-falls)

For a buyer that means: look at what the bike cost in **its own year**. A seller who works from their old, higher purchase price will easily ask too much.

**Worked example.** A 2022 Topstone 1 (€2,449 new) in normal used condition is worth about **€1,130 to €1,380** after four years, according to our [value estimator](/value).

## What to check

- **1x or 2x**: one chainring (1x) is simpler, two (2x) give more range. It's often in the name.
- **Wheel size**: the Topstone can run 700c and smaller 650b wheels. Ask what's fitted.
- **Is it really a Topstone Carbon?** The aluminium and carbon frames look alike in photos. The [check](/) puts the ad's parts next to the original and tells which it is.
