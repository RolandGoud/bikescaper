The groupset is the most useful handle in an ad. Brands build their price ladder on it, so if you know the groupset, you roughly know what the bike cost new and what frame and wheels come with it. Bikecheck rates every groupset on five tiers, the same for every brand.

## The five tiers

| Tier | Shimano | SRAM | Typical frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| {tier:1} | Claris, Sora | | aluminium or steel |
| {tier:2} | Tiagra, CUES | Apex | aluminium |
| {tier:3} | 105 | Rival | entry carbon |
| {tier:4} | Ultegra | Force | performance carbon |
| {tier:5} | Dura-Ace | Red | the brand's top carbon |


Gravel bikes use Shimano GRX, with its own numbers (400, 600, 800) that sit roughly next to Tiagra, 105 and Ultegra. **Di2** (Shimano) or **AXS** (SRAM) after the name means electronic shifting.

What a bike costs new at each tier is in [what a groupset really costs you](/blog/groupset-new-prices).

## Groupsets age

This is where ads often go wrong. Shimano and SRAM bring out a new generation about every four to five years, and each one passes its features a step down the range. Today's 105 shifts like the Ultegra of a generation ago: 12 speeds, hydraulic disc brakes, and even electronic if you want.

So Bikecheck drops a groupset **one tier for every five years** since its model year, at most two tiers and never below Basic. A 2015 road bike with Ultegra (Performance) now counts as Entry. The spec sheet shows both: its tier when new and its tier now.

## What to look for in an ad

- **Does the groupset match the model?** Look the model up in the [catalogue](/models). If the ad names a different groupset than the factory build, ask what was replaced.
- **Mixed groupsets** (a 105 derailleur with Tiagra shifters) are very normal after repairs, but lower the value.
- **Mechanical or electronic?** On an older Di2 or AXS bike, ask about the battery. A new one costs money.
- **How many speeds?** 9, 10, 11 or 12 at the back says a lot about age. Parts for 9- and 10-speed are getting scarcer.

## Upgrade or not?

Upgrading is often dearer than you'd think, because more needs replacing than just the derailleur. [Groupset upgrade paths](/groupsets) shows for each groupset what can replace it, what else has to change and whether it's worth it.
