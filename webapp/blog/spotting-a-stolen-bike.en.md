A €4,000 road bike for sale at €900 isn't a lucky find. It's one of the clearest signs that something is off. And if you buy a stolen bike, you don't just lose it when it's traced: according to [the Dutch police](https://www.politie.nl/informatie/heling-is-strafbaar.html), handling stolen goods (heling) is a crime even if you only *could have suspected* the bike was stolen.

## The price: suspiciously cheap

For every ad, Bikecheck works out a fair range from the new price, the age and the condition. If the asking price is **below 60% of the bottom of that range**, we call it *suspiciously cheap* and add a warning. A slightly low price is just a good deal; a price far below it needs an explanation.

## The signs

The [police](https://www.politie.nl/informatie/hoe-weet-ik-of-de-tweedehands-fiets-die-ik-koop-niet-gestolen-is.html) and the [Fietsersbond](https://www.fietsersbond.nl/veilig-fietsen/fietsdiefstal/preventie/koop-geen-gestolen-fiets/) keep naming the same things:

- **No receipt**, or none with the frame number on it.
- **The seller knows little about the bike**: no idea of the size, year or groupset.
- **Won't meet at home**, but at a station or car park, preferably quickly.
- **Marks on the bike**: a forced lock, a scratched-off dealer sticker, grinding marks where a postcode or frame number was.
- **A size that doesn't fit the seller**, or several expensive bikes for sale at once.

## Check the frame number

The frame number is usually under the bottom bracket. Look it up before you pay:

- The [RDW](https://www.rdw.nl/kopen-of-verkopen/controleren-of-een-fiets-gestolen-is) shows whether the bike was reported stolen. Note: "not registered" doesn't mean the bike is clean.
- [BIKEBAZE](https://www.bikebaze.nl/blog/fiets-checken-of-die-gestolen-is-doe-het-direct-via-bikebaze) searches its own register, Stop Heling and the international Bike Index in one go.
- In Belgium, use [MyBike](https://mybike.belgium.be/nl/).

## How to buy safely

1. Paste the ad into the [check](/) and see whether the price is fair and the story fits the model.
2. Ask for a receipt with the frame number and an ID, and compare the number with the bike.
3. Meet at the seller's address.
4. Write a sales agreement with name, address, frame number and price, and keep it.
