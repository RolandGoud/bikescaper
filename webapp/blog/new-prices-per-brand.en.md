If you buy a used road bike, you first need to know what that bike cost new. Whatever a seller asks is a percentage of that. That's why Bikecheck tracks the brands' own new prices: our [catalogue](/models) now holds **1,136 road, gravel and time trial bikes with a current Dutch new price**, from 16 brands.

## What stands out

**The brand tells you less than you think.** Nearly every big brand has bikes under €1,500 and over €12,000. A "Specialized" in an ad can be a €1,499 Allez or a €15,000 S-Works Tarmac. The model and above all the groupset set the price, not the logo on the down tube.

**Giant, Cube and Rose are the value brands.** Their medians sit around €3,300 to €3,900, while Specialized, Canyon and BMC sit around €5,500 to €6,000. Giant often gives you the same groupset for less, and that carries over to the used market: the asking price follows the lower new price down.

**Factor, Wilier and Cervélo are niche.** Few entry models, lots of top bikes. You see them less often second-hand, and then usually at a high price.

## What this means when you buy used

An asking price only means something next to the new price of **exactly that model and model year**. Two Giants from the same year can have cost €2,000 or €12,000 new. Look the model up in the [catalogue](/models), or let the [value estimator](/value) do the sums: it takes the new price, takes off for age and condition, and gives you a fair range.
