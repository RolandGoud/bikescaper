The Endurace is Canyon's endurance road bike: a little more upright than the race bikes, with room for wider tyres, made for long days on any road. Because Canyon sells only online, at sharp prices, it's a popular bike, and plenty of them come up second-hand. Our catalogue has **31 carbon Endurace builds** from 2018 to today, plus the aluminium ones.

## CF, CF SL, CF SLX, CFR

Canyon puts the frame's carbon grade in the name:

- **Endurace** or **Endurace AL**, no CF: aluminium.
- **CF** is the entry carbon frame.
- **CF SL** was the middle grade. You'll see it in many ads, but it's not in Canyon's current range.
- **CF SLX** is the lighter, stiffer frame.
- **CFR** is the top frame, Canyon's lightest.

The number after it is the build, and it follows the groupset: **6** is Tiagra, **7** is 105 (with Di2 in the name: 105 Di2) or SRAM Rival, **8** is Ultegra or SRAM Force, **9** is Dura-Ace or SRAM Red. So a CF SL 8 Di2 is a mid-grade frame with Ultegra Di2.

## What to check on a used Endurace

- **Rim or disc brakes?** Older Endurace bikes came in both, and "Disc" in the name was the disc version. Check the photos: rim-brake bikes are fine, but sell for less.
- **The paperwork.** Canyon sells direct, so every first owner has an order confirmation with the model, year and size. Ask for it: it also shows the bike is the seller's.
- **The size.** Canyon goes from 3XS to 2XL, and an M is meant for 177 to 184 cm, larger than at many brands. Check yours in the [size finder](/size). Our [guide to frame sizes](/blog/frame-size-from-an-ad) explains why.
- **The groupset.** Same name, different year, different parts. Paste the ad into the [check](/) and we put the parts next to that year's original.
