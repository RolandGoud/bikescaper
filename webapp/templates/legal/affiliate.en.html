How we make money

Bikecheck is free. To pay for the site we show a few sponsored links: offers on the home page and between the bikes under Listings and Our catalogue, and the shops on the groupset pages. If you buy something through such a link, the shop pays us a small fee. It costs you nothing extra.

You can tell a sponsored link by the AD · Sponsored label, or by the button to a shop on a groupset page. The links go through affiliate networks such as Daisycon, TradeTracker and Awin.

What that doesn't change

  • Checks, scores, value estimates and size advice come from the same calculation for every bike and every brand. No advertiser can change them or pay for them.
  • We're not affiliated with Marktplaats, 2dehands or any bike brand.
  • No advertiser scripts, pixels or cookies run on our pages. Only when you click a sponsored link does the network track that click, on its side. More in our privacy statement.
